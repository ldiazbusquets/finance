About

About this site

An independent finance site by Luis Diaz Busquets, a finance student at Bryant University with minors in Applied AI and Law.

Why it exists

Most research hides its assumptions. Here, every number links back to where it came from and every model shows its inputs, so you can disagree with a conclusion and see exactly where.

The site sits alongside the main site, which covers who I am and my experience, and LDB Lab, which is about machine learning.

Main site
Where the data comes from
Market data
Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Treasury yields, the policy rate, inflation, jobs, credit spreads, oil, the dollar and the S&P 500.
Company figures
SEC EDGAR: the financial statements companies file in their 10-K annual reports.
How often
An automatic job refreshes everything every weekday evening after US markets close. Some series, like inflation, are only published monthly.
