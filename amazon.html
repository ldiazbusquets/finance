01 · The business

A store, a cloud and an ad network.

Amazon reports three segments. North America and International are the store: first-party retail, third-party seller services, Prime subscriptions and advertising. AWS rents computing, storage and AI infrastructure to companies and governments.

Most of the profit comes from AWS and advertising, which ride on the store’s traffic. Retail itself earns thin margins but funds the logistics network that keeps customers loyal. Amazon also owns a large stake in Anthropic, the AI company, which it does not value in its operating results.

Takeaway. Value comes mostly from AWS and ads; the store is the moat that feeds them.

Second-quarter revenue rose 20% to $200.6 billion. AWS revenue grew 37% to $42.2 billion with $16.6 billion of operating income, and advertising grew 26%. Net income of $62.6 billion was swollen by a $53.4 billion gain, mostly from revaluing the Anthropic stake, so it says little about the business.

The cost is in cash flow: free cash flow over the last twelve months was negative $7.6 billion, because purchases of property and equipment rose $66 billion in a year. Capital spending for 2026 is guided to about $220 billion. Guidance for the third quarter is $197–202 billion of revenue and $22.5–26.5 billion of operating income.

04 · Balance sheet

Strong, but now borrowing to build.

Amazon held about $123 billion of cash against $133 billion of debt in June, so net debt is small. It pays no dividend and buys back little; every spare dollar goes into data centers, chips (Trainium) and the Leo satellite network. The Anthropic stake is a sizeable asset that is not included in this valuation, so it is upside to the numbers below.

06 · What the price assumes

At $263, the build-out has to earn a lot.

To reach today’s price, you needInstead of
A long-run operating margin near 29%18%; Amazon’s best year so far was about 12%
Revenue growth of about 23% a year for two years12% and 11%
A discount rate of about 7.9%10.8%

The market is betting that the $220 billion a year going into AI infrastructure earns AWS-like returns. The Anthropic stake, left out of the model, covers part of the gap.

What could go wrong, and what could go right.

Consumer slowdownRetail and ads both depend on spending.

Upside: Trainium chips cutting AWS costs, advertising in Prime Video, Leo satellite internet and the value of the Anthropic stake.

08 · Recommendation

Hold. A great company in an expensive building phase.

At $262.63 against a base case of $153 ($162 weighted), the shares price in much higher long-run margins than Amazon has ever earned. The call is Hold: keep the core position, don’t add until free cash flow turns back up or the price comes closer to the bull case.

Watch in the third-quarter report: AWS growth staying above 30%, operating income against the $22.5–26.5 billion guide, and any change to the $220 billion capex plan.

Final view

Own the platform, wait for the cash.

The spending is a bet on AI that only pays off if AWS keeps its margins as it grows.
