OpenAI

OpenAI makes ChatGPT, used by more than a billion people a week. It is the most valuable private company in AI and is raising money at about $1.4 trillion, but it also expects to burn hundreds of billions of dollars before it turns a profit. An IPO is planned for 2027.

01 · The business

ChatGPT, and everything built on it.

OpenAI sells ChatGPT subscriptions to consumers and businesses, API access to developers, and since 2026 advertising inside ChatGPT. It converted from a capped-profit structure into a public benefit corporation, OpenAI Group PBC, in October 2025. The nonprofit OpenAI Foundation controls it and owns about a quarter; Microsoft owns about 27%.

OpenAI spends far more on computing than it earns. Its plan relies on partners (Microsoft, Oracle, SoftBank’s Stargate venture) financing much of the infrastructure, and on raising new money roughly every year until revenue catches up.

04 · Funding and ownership

$852 billion in March, $1.4 trillion in talks.

In March 2026 OpenAI closed a $122 billion round at $852 billion, anchored by Amazon ($50 billion), Nvidia ($30 billion) and SoftBank ($30 billion). In October it was reported to be raising about $30 billion more at a valuation above $1.4 trillion, before Anthropic’s IPO. Sam Altman has said listing in 2026 would be ill-advised; the IPO is expected in 2027.

08 · Our view

Not investable yet; fair near $850 billion.

Until the 2027 IPO there is no way to buy OpenAI directly. The base case supports roughly the March valuation; the $1.4 trillion round prices in more than the base case. Indirect exposure already comes through Microsoft, Nvidia, Oracle and Amazon.

Final view

The biggest audience in AI, the biggest bill.

Its value depends on turning a billion users into profit before the money runs out.
