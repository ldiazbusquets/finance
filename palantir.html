01 · The business

One platform, two kinds of customer.

Palantir builds software that joins up an organisation’s data and lets people, and now AI models, act on it. Gotham and Maven serve defence and intelligence agencies; Foundry does the same for companies; AIP, launched in 2023, puts large language models to work inside both. Customers usually start with a short “bootcamp” and expand from there.

Government is still the larger half. In the U.S. it includes the Army’s enterprise agreement of up to $10 billion over ten years, signed in 2025. The faster-growing half is U.S. commercial, where AIP has turned Palantir from a slow-moving consultant-heavy business into one that sells like software.

The second quarter, reported on August 3, beat estimates and brought the largest guidance raise in the company’s history: 2026 revenue of about $8.15 billion, up about 82%, with U.S. commercial revenue above $3.4 billion. Adjusted free cash flow was $1.22 billion in the quarter, 63% of revenue, and the “Rule of 40” score (growth plus margin) reached 155%. U.S. commercial contracts signed in the quarter were worth a record $2.1 billion.

For the third quarter Palantir guided to revenue of $2.16 billion, about 12% more than the second. The shares reached a record $212.94 on October 12, then fell 7% on October 16 with other AI software names, with no company news. Results come on November 2.

04 · Balance sheet and ownership

About $9.4 billion of cash and no debt.

At the end of June Palantir held about $9.4 billion of cash and short-term Treasuries and had no borrowings. Dilution from stock pay is the main cost to shareholders: this model assumes about 2.6 billion diluted shares by 2030, roughly 1% a year more than today. Co-founders hold super-voting shares, so outside shareholders have little say over the company.

05 · Valuation

Our scenarios give $87 a share.

Valued like the other fast growers on this site: revenue and operating margin in 2030, taxed at 21%, times an exit multiple, discounted back four years at 14% (beta 2.0 gives a CAPM cost of about 14.2%), plus net cash and the free cash flow earned along the way (a straight line from about $3.4 billion over the last twelve months to 80% of 2030 after-tax profit).

08 · Rating

Sell. The best numbers in software, priced as if they last.

Few software companies have ever grown this fast at this size while earning these margins. The price matches the model’s bull case: it needs about 50% growth a year for four years at a record margin.

What would change the value: Several more quarters of 80%-plus U.S. commercial growth would lift the base case; any slowdown would hit a price with no room for one. Third-quarter results come on November 2.

Ranges, not three cases. The expected value is the average of 4,000 runs of this report’s own model, with every input drawn from a range: the base case is the middle, and the bear and bull cases are the 10th and 90th percentiles. Business inputs move together; the discount rate moves on its own. The 25/50/25 weighting in the valuation section gives $107. How the ranges work

Two different questions. The rating is relative: it asks whether the stock is cheaper or dearer than the S&P 500 on the same assumptions. The absolute view asks whether it is worth its price at all: on its own the stock trades above our estimated value, which is 47% below the price.

On the same assumptions every report uses (a 5.15% Treasury yield and a 4.5% equity premium), the S&P 500 itself is worth about 68% of its price. The rating compares each stock with that: Buy if it is at least 15% cheaper than the market on this basis, Sell if at least 15% dearer, Hold in between. It is the same rule for every company, whoever owns it. The rating depends on that assumption: it would be Hold if the index grows 6% a year and Sell at 10%, against 8% here. How ratings work

Against the Street: The analysts’ average of about $206 is close to the price. The gap with this model is mostly the exit multiple (30× base against the 45× or more the price implies) and a 14% discount rate from Palantir’s beta of about 2.
