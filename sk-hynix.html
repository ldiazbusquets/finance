01 · The business

The leader in AI memory.

SK hynix makes DRAM, the working memory in every computer, and NAND flash for storage. Its edge is HBM: high-bandwidth memory made by stacking DRAM chips, which Nvidia and other AI chipmakers place next to each accelerator. SK hynix was first to supply HBM at scale to Nvidia and held about 50% of the market in the second quarter, ahead of Samsung (33%) and Micron (18%). It began mass shipments of the newest generation, HBM4, for Nvidia’s Vera Rubin platform this year.

It is the same industry as Micron, with the same cycle: three companies make nearly all the world’s DRAM, and prices rise and fall with the balance between their new factories and demand.

Second-quarter revenue rose 257% from a year earlier to ₩79.3 trillion and operating profit 557% to ₩60.5 trillion, a 76% margin. Net profit of ₩93.9 trillion was even higher than operating profit because of large gains outside its operations. HBM4 mass shipments began in the quarter and ramp through the second half; the company has signed long-term agreements of about five years, with deposits and volume commitments, with about ten customers.

Korean analysts expect third-quarter operating profit of about ₩77 trillion, a sixth straight record, as DRAM prices rose about 22% and NAND about 15% in the quarter. Results come on October 29 in Seoul (the evening of October 28 in New York). On October 22 Bernstein estimated that Samsung overtook SK hynix in HBM revenue in the third quarter, $8.7 billion to $7.6 billion, and the ADRs fell about 8%.

04 · Balance sheet and supply

₩69 trillion of net cash, new fabs from 2027.

At the end of June SK hynix had ₩88 trillion of cash against ₩18.6 trillion of debt. In July it listed ADRs on the Nasdaq under SKHY, issuing up to 17.79 million new shares (about 2.4% more) for up to about $29 billion; this model adds that to net cash, about $80 billion in all. Capital spending will reach the high ₩40 trillions this year (about $35 billion). New capacity comes from the M15X fab, pulled forward, and the first Yongin cleanroom in early 2027.

Management says it does not expect its investment plans to cause oversupply soon and that tight conditions should last “for a considerable period”. It is weighing more shareholder returns, with analysts expecting buybacks of more than ₩30 trillion.

08 · Recommendation

Watch. The best position in memory, at peak conditions.

SK hynix earned the AI memory boom: first to HBM at scale, the largest share of Nvidia’s supply and a 76% margin. Compared with Micron it looks cheaper, only about 6% above our weighted value against Micron’s 27%. But it is still a memory company at the top of its cycle, Samsung is catching up in HBM, and its home currency adds a risk Micron does not have. At $131.62 we would watch the October 29 results; nearer $110, our base case, it would be the better of the two memory makers to own.

Final view

The HBM leader, closer to fair than its rivals.

The cycle still decides; the price leaves less to go wrong than Micron’s.
