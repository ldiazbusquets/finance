Every call this site publishes is logged on the day it is made and never edited. Here is how each one has done since, against the S&P 500 over the same days.

Every call

Return from the price on the day of the report to the last close, against the S&P 500 over the same period. A Buy is right if it beats the index; a Hold or Watch is judged on whether the price moved toward the report’s value.

The log started on October 20, 2026, so early results mostly reflect a few days of noise. Private-company views are not scored because they have no market price. Costco (FIN 370 coursework) is left out because its sections were priced on different dates. Not investment advice.
