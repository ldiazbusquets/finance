01 · The business

Keeping AI chips powered and cool.

Vertiv sells uninterruptible power supplies, switchgear and power distribution, thermal management (including liquid cooling for GPUs), and the service that keeps them running. Its customers are cloud companies, colocation operators and enterprises building data centers, now increasingly for AI.

As each rack of AI chips draws more power and generates more heat, the equipment Vertiv sells per megawatt rises. Its main competitors are Schneider Electric and Eaton.

Second-quarter sales rose 24% to $3.27 billion and adjusted EPS rose 60%, but organic growth of 17.8% fell short of the 20–24% the company itself had guided. Management blamed the timing of multi-phase projects and supply-chain sequencing and declined to size the shortfall; the stock fell 17% that day. It has also stopped disclosing orders and backlog.

At the same time it raised full-year guidance: sales of $13.8–14.2 billion, 30–32% organic growth and adjusted EPS of $6.65–6.75. That implies about 40% organic growth in the second half, which the third-quarter report will test.

04 · Balance sheet

From leveraged to net cash.

Vertiv was formed from a private-equity buyout and carried heavy debt. It now holds about $2.8 billion of cash against $2.9 billion of debt, essentially net cash neutral, with $5.6 billion of liquidity. It is spending on bolt-on acquisitions in liquid cooling and services.

07 · Risks

What could go wrong, and what could go right.

RiskWhy it matters
ExecutionOne quarter already missed its own range; another would hurt credibility further.
VisibilityWith orders and backlog no longer disclosed, demand is harder to check.
Data-center spendingResults depend on a small number of very large customers’ build plans.
CompetitionSchneider, Eaton and new liquid-cooling entrants are chasing the same projects.

Upside: a third quarter that delivers the guided 34–36% organic growth would show the miss was timing, and the stock is already 46% below its high.

08 · Recommendation

Hold. Down a lot, still not cheap at its risk.

At $203.56 against a base case of $117 ($122 weighted), Vertiv is fully valued at its high cost of capital, though the bull case ($188) is close to the price. The call is Hold; add only after a clean third quarter.

Final view

The right market, prove the execution.

Demand is not the issue; delivering it on schedule is.
